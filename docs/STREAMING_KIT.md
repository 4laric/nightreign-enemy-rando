## Stream-setup notes (not viewer-facing)

- **Show the seed.** The mod has no in-game seed display — it's a file
  mod, the game doesn't know it's randomized. Put the seed in the stream
  title or the "Seeds" panel every session, or chat can't race it.
- **Keep the GUI one alt-tab away.** The reroll loop — new seed →
  Randomize → relaunch through me3 — is what you'll do live when a seed
  misbehaves. Practice it once so a CTD is a 60-second bit, not dead
  air.
- **A break on stream is a free bug report.** Note the seed and the VOD
  timestamp when something hangs or crashes — that's a reproducible,
  recorded repro you can act on later.
- **Clip in the moment.** A marquee boss landing somewhere absurd, an
  Oops! All reveal, a genuinely cursed chimera — clip it live. Those
  clips are your Nexus-page and Reddit content; streaming and the launch
  push feed each other.

## Format ideas

- **Blind randomizer runs** — lowest prep, every arena is a reveal.
- **Oops! All [chat votes]** — chat picks one enemy, you run Oops! All
  that. Pure interactive bit, and the clip titles write themselves.
- **Randomized Nightlord gauntlet** — beat every Nightlord on randomized
  seeds as an episodic series with an on-screen tracker. This one earns
  the most: it gives viewers a reason to come back next stream.

Lead the launch with blind runs or Oops! All (low prep, high reaction);
use the gauntlet as the recurring backbone.
